Here are the main ways to find documentation for a symbol:

* **In a notebook:** select the symbol and press F1.
* **From code:** evaluate `?Plot` for a short usage message, or `??Plot` for attributes and options.
* **Online:** visit the [Wolfram Language Documentation Center](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/).
* **Programmatically:** use `Information["Plot"]` or `Options[Plot]`.
