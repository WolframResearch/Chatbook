Formatting combinations:

- ***bold italic with asterisks***
- ___bold italic with underscores___
- **bold** and __also bold__
- *italic* and _also italic_
- ~~strikethrough~~
- \textbf{LaTeX bold} and \textit{LaTeX italic}
- **bold with `code` inside**
- *italic with [a link](https://example.com) inside*
- **[bold link](paclet:ref/Plot)**
- [**link with bold label**](https://example.com/bold)
- snake_case_names and file_name_v2.txt should not be italic
- 2*3*4 is multiplication, and a * b * c too
- An asterisk at the end of a word*
- Emphasis spanning
  two lines **should
  not** be bold

Within a sentence: the **`Plot`** function and *`ListPlot`* are similar, but __`Show`__ combines them.
